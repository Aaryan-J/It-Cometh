"You are working so hard... just to die completely exhausted."

IT COMETH is a low-res, retro-analog survival horror game. Trapped deep inside a grimy, concrete fallback bunker, you are not alone. An ancient cosmic entity approaches from the expanse, and its sheer physical proximity is actively compressing reality. Your remaining time is your only metric of distance. When your clock strikes zero... it has arrived.

## SECURE SECTOR EMERGENCY

The entity has stripped all of your machinery components of their features, reducing them to visual black boxes. When a critical system fails, you must explore the dark using your flashlight and hover your crosshair over them to identify which system requires attention.

Keeping the bunker systems functional pushes the compression boundaries back, completely refilling your remaining lifespan cap. However, each repair takes a toll on the structure, your maximum time capacity will shrink by 10 seconds after every fix.

- THE FUSE BOX: Circuit overloads will plunge the bunker into absolute pitch-black darkness. Match the colored nodes perfectly to complete the grid loop and restore power.
- THE RADIO: Drag the dial needle until the sound stabilizes and locks onto the target frequency threshold. Release the mouse button immediately when you reach the target frequency to anchor the transmission.
- OXYGEN FILTER: When the atmospheric vents clog, the O2 bar will violently fluctuate. Rapidly click the vent button to pump the pressure down, balancing the gauge needle strictly within the 40% to 60% safe zone for 3 consecutive seconds.
- MAINFRAME LOCKOUT: When the central computer crashes, the terminal interface completely locks down. You must drop what you are doing, leave the console, and explore the grimy bunker corridors to locate three handwritten sticky notes hidden randomly on the walls. Input all three 4-digit sequences in any order to bypass the lock.

## CRITICAL ANOMALY ALERT

The bunker monitors local spatial distortions. If the emergency display flashes a red warning and the broadcast audio commands: "DO NOT LOOK BEHIND YOU"... FREEZE YOUR CAMERA IMMEDIATELY.

Do not look back. Do not glance around. Never look behind. Never. It gets angry...

## CONTROLS

- [WASD] — Navigate the Bunker
- [Mouse] — Look / Aim Perspective
- [F] — Toggle Flashlight On/Off
- [E] — Interact with Black Boxes / Read Sticky Notes
- [Left Click] — Interact with Minigame UI Elements
- [ESC] — Unlock Mouse Cursor (Main Menu)
